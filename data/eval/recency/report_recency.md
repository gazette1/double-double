Sample weight 0.5 ** (age / H) with age in seasons from the latest training season; W3 keeps only the last three training seasons. The holdout (last 15 percent of training rows by date) is unweighted and drives early stopping and Platt calibration. V1_isotonic is the saved v1 C_full prediction with its isotonic calibration, for scale; H_inf is the same model refit here with Platt.
