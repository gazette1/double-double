## Setup

- Folds, rows and holdout slices are the v1 ones: test seasons 2023 to 2026 (season end year), training on all earlier seasons from 2018, the last 15 percent of training rows by date (cut on a date boundary) as the holdout slice H, and the earlier training rows as the fit rows F. Metrics use regular-season test rows. The test season is never used for fitting.
- A, C_full and C_noabs test outputs come from the v1 predictions file. Their holdout-slice outputs, needed for the blends and the calibration study, are scored with the saved v1 boosters and isotonic calibrators in `data/models/v1/`; the saved C_full booster reproduces the v1 test predictions exactly (maximum absolute difference 0.0e+00).
- MIN: LightGBM minutes models with the v1 LightGBM settings (num_leaves 31, learning_rate 0.03, up to 3000 rounds with early stopping 100 on H, min_child_samples 100, feature_fraction 0.8, bagging_fraction 0.8, bagging_freq 1, lambda_l2 5, seed 42, deterministic). The point model uses objective regression_l1, so its prediction is the conditional median (q50); quantile models at 0.1 and 0.9 use the same features. MIN_full uses all 155 v1 features, MIN_noabs drops the 7 abs_ features. The target is the current game's minutes.
- SIM: for each row a grid of 15 equal-probability minutes quantiles of a split normal with median q50, lower standard deviation (q50 - q10) / 1.2816 and upper standard deviation (q90 - q50) / 1.2816, truncated to [0, 48] (quantile levels spread evenly between the distribution function at 0 and at 48). Per-minute rates for points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks: the v1 features do not store the EWM minutes weight, so the spec's fallback is used, rate = (l20 rate * w + prior * 300) / (w + 300) with w = min(games in the l20 window, 20) * min_l20. Position priors are per-minute league rates by ESPN position on the fit rows F. Given minutes m, points, rebounds and assists are negative binomial with mean rate * m and variance-to-mean ratios 2.78, 1.29 and 1.17; steals and blocks are Poisson. P(dd | m) = 1 - P(no stat reaches 10) - P(exactly one does), averaged over the grid with equal weights. SIM uses the MIN_full quantiles, SIM_noabs the MIN_noabs ones. SIM is reported raw and with isotonic calibration fitted on H.
- C_stack: the C_full features plus sim_p (raw SIM) and min_pred (MIN_full q50), trained like C_full (LightGBM binary on F, early stopping on H). C_stack_noabs uses the C_noabs features plus sim_p_noabs and min_pred_noabs. Calibrated outputs use the Platt method chosen in the calibration study.
- BLEND_A: sigmoid(w * logit(C_full calibrated) + (1 - w) * logit(Baseline A calibrated)), with w on the grid 0.00 to 1.00 in steps of 0.05 chosen by log loss on H. BLEND_SIM uses calibrated SIM in place of Baseline A. Calibrated inputs are the v1 isotonic outputs (and the SIM isotonic outputs); on H these are in-sample, because the calibrators were fitted on H. Probabilities are clipped to [0.0001, 0.9999] before the logit, the same clipping log loss uses.
- Calibration study: isotonic (the v1 calibrator), Platt (logistic regression on logit p) and beta calibration (logistic regression on log p and -log(1 - p), with both slopes kept non-negative), each fitted on H and applied to the test season, for C_full raw outputs, then for C_stack and C_stack_noabs.
- Proxy-edge rule (an assumption, not a price backtest): described in its section below.
- Runtime: 17.6 minutes from start to this report (four folds, metrics, report).

### Leakage discipline

- MIN target: the current game's minutes. MIN features: the v1 feature list, which uses only games before the current date; the abs_ features (MIN_full only) are lineup-known, as in C_full. The script asserts that no current-game outcome column is a feature.
- SIM rates use pre-game l20 features. The only fitted SIM inputs are the position priors (outcomes of fit rows) and the MIN quantiles; the variance-to-mean ratios are fixed constants from the spec.
- C_stack training rows (F) get out-of-fold sim_p and min_pred. F is split into 3 contiguous date blocks. Each block is scored by MIN models (both variants, all three quantiles) and position priors fitted on the other blocks of F only. The number of boosting rounds of these inner models is fixed at the main MIN model's early-stopping iteration, which was chosen on H, so no block's own minutes or outcomes enter its sim_p, min_pred, or round count. Rows in later blocks do contain earlier blocks' minutes inside their pre-game window features, but only as inputs, never as targets, so they cannot carry a row's own target into its prediction.
- C_stack holdout and test rows get sim_p and min_pred from the main MIN models fitted on F alone. The rows used for early stopping and calibration (H) and the test rows are therefore produced the same way, and neither was used to fit the MIN models (H only sets their early-stopping round).
- Calibrators, SIM isotonic maps and blend weights are fitted on H. The calibration method applied to C_stack is chosen without test-season outcomes: each method is fitted on the first half of each fold's H (by date) and scored on the second half, pooled over the four folds.
- Out-of-fold quality check (diagnostics table below), averaged over folds: MIN_full median MAE 4.58 minutes on F from the inner models versus 4.62 on H from the main models; raw SIM log loss 0.1942 on F versus 0.1962 on H. F and H cover different dates, so these are a check on scale, not an exact comparison.

## Proxy-edge rule (an assumption, not a price backtest)

This section rests on an assumption about prices; no historical double-double prices were available. The assumed stand-in implied probability is q = min(0.97, 1.08 * Baseline A calibrated p), meant to mimic a Yes-only price with about 8 percent margin. A Yes bet is placed when model calibrated p minus q is at least the threshold. Returns assume a flat 1-unit stake paid at the fair price of q: return per bet = hit * (1 - q) / q - (1 - hit). Baseline A is listed for completeness: q is 1.08 times its calibrated p (capped at 0.97) and its largest calibrated p in these rows is 0.944, so its p never exceeds q by a threshold and it places no bets. Forward prices are logged in `data/odds/dd_prices.csv` for the real test; until that sample exists these figures say only how each model disagrees with Baseline A, not whether it beats a book. Baseline A has higher log loss than every model here (metric 1), and the bets are rows where Baseline A is low relative to the model; metric 5 shows the realized rate on such rows above Baseline A's mean (C_stack more than 0.10 above A: realized 0.302 versus A mean 0.186, n = 3747). Positive returns under this assumption are therefore expected by construction. The median American price of the bets sits far down the longshot range, so a few hits at long implied odds move the mean return.

## Notes and deviations from the spec

- Inner out-of-fold split: 3 time-ordered blocks of the fit rows instead of the 5 suggested in the task. The choice was made before the full run from a timing test on the largest fold (about 30 seconds per median minutes fit), which projected about 35 minutes in total for 5 blocks against the spec's 30-minute budget. In the measured run the 3-block inner fits took 10.8 minutes; 5 blocks would take about 2.0 times as long, about 28 minutes in total, inside the budget with about 2 minutes of margin. `--inner-folds 5` runs that version. Each block's inner models train on 67 percent of F; the diagnostics table compares their quality with the main models.
- Metric 1 scores C_stack with Platt and C_full with the v1 isotonic calibration, as the spec directs. The isotonic step raises C_full's pooled log loss from 0.1892 to 0.1899, so the same-calibration table is the measure of what sim_p and min_pred add.
- BLEND_A and BLEND_SIM blend the v1 isotonic C_full, as the spec directs; both blends have higher pooled log loss than Platt-calibrated C_full (BLEND_A +0.00050, BLEND_SIM +0.00020). The weight on C_full is at least 0.60 in every fold.
- The proxy-edge figures (an assumption, not a price backtest) use Baseline A as the stand-in market, and Baseline A has higher log loss than every other model here, so they measure disagreement with a weak reference.
- The EWM minutes weight is not stored in the v1 features, so the SIM rates use the spec's fallback: l20 per-minute rates with weight min(games in the window, 20) * min_l20. The EWM sum of weights could be rebuilt from games_played_career (about 12 games at most for half-life 8), but the spec designates the l20 fallback for this case.
- Steals and blocks have no stored per-minute features; their l20 rates are stl_l20 / min_l20 and blk_l20 / min_l20, equal to the ratio of window sums because both are means over the same games.
- The variance-to-mean ratios (2.78, 1.29, 1.17) were measured on raw game logs within player-seasons, so they include minutes variation. SIM applies them conditional on minutes and also mixes over minutes, so its spread is wider than the measured one; the isotonic step corrects the level, not the ranking.
- Position priors are computed on the fit rows F (the rows the MIN models are fitted on), and on F minus the block for the out-of-fold rows.
- The minutes grid is the truncated split normal described in Setup. When separately fitted quantile models cross (q10 above q50 or q90 below q50), each half-width is floored at 0.25 minutes; crossing rates are in the minutes section.
- C_full calibrated outputs in metric 1 stay the v1 isotonic ones, so they match `report_v1.md`. Platt and beta versions are in the calibration study. The blends use the v1 isotonic C_full.
- The calibration method for C_stack is chosen on holdout halves, not on test seasons, so that C_stack's reported calibrated scores carry no test-season selection.
- The proxy-edge returns are an assumption-based illustration, not a backtest against prices. Under the assumption, q goes as low as 0.0056 because Baseline A calibrated p goes that low, and a few longshot hits can dominate the mean return; the posted-range table restricts q to 1/51 or more.
- The optional v2 features (arena_ast_excess_prev and the abs_ season-boundary fix) were not built. Both change the C_full feature set, so they need a rebuilt dataset and retrained C_full and C_noabs to report metric 7, which does not fit next to this run in the 30-minute budget; v1 predictions were kept as the comparison point as the spec asks.
- Metrics 2, 3, 6 and 7 of v1 are not repeated; the calibration study covers calibration for the models that changed, and metric 7 is unchanged because C_full and C_noabs are the v1 models.
- Baseline B is kept in the predictions file but not in the v2 tables, which follow the spec's model list.
