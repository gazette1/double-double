- Metrics use regular-season rows (season_type 2) of the test season unless stated. Playoff and play-in rows are scored and saved in the predictions file but excluded from the tables.
- Baseline A: p = (dd_count_season + 10 * prior) / (gp_season + 10). prior = previous season dd rate, else the player's career dd rate before this season, else the league dd rate of the training seasons. Isotonic calibration fitted on the calibration slice.

## Notes and deviations from the spec

- All-Star and Rising Stars games (17 games) are coded as season_type 2 with non-franchise team ids; they are dropped before any feature is computed.
- Second ESPN athlete ids for the same player in the same game were dropped (31 played rows, mostly 2018-19). The spec's (game_id, athlete_id) dedup alone does not catch them; the raw files have no exact (game_id, athlete_id) duplicates.
- Game 401161536 was dropped: a corrupted timestamp put two Golden State games on 2020-03-01.
- The six 2020-21 play-in games are missing from team_box and have no played rows in the player box. They count for scores and schedule features and are skipped inside box-stat windows.
- The team rank features (team_reb_rank, team_ast_rank) rank the player among the team's pre-game regulars, not among the players who played the game, so that C_noabs carries no lineup information.
- Regulars exclude players whose latest played game before the current date was for another team (traded or signed elsewhere), following the spec's word teammates.
- The abs_ regular window runs across seasons as the spec's N-game rule implies. In a team's first games of a season it counts departed regulars as absent (mean abs_n_absent_regulars 2.60 in game 1 versus 0.90 from game 6 on).
- Team possessions use the turnovers column of team_box (player turnovers); total_turnovers is missing for 2017-18. Opponent allowed rebounds use player-credited rebounds (oreb + dreb), not total_rebounds, which includes team rebounds.
- team_days_rest is capped at 10 like the player rest feature.
- Baseline A and Baseline B calibration use the same holdout slice as Model C (the spec calls it the training slice for Baseline A).
- The league-rate fallback of Baseline A uses all training rows of the fold (rows with at least 5 earlier games).
- Games played career and the 5-game filter count games since 2017-18 only, the start of the data.
